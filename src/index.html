Observed activity

Sources: UN Global Platform; IMF PortWatch (portwatch.imf.org). Network and economic-model sources: University of Oxford; IMF PortWatch.

Daily AIS-derived port calls, transit calls and estimated trade/capacity. Ports and passages count different events; do not sum their calls as unique ships. Tonnes are estimates, not customs declarations or trade value. AIS gaps, spoofing and revisions can affect observations. Zero recorded activity is not proof of no physical traffic.

Current means require every day in the selected window. Baselines require 28 observations and must finish before the current window starts. Missing or incomplete windows are excluded from change rankings. Percentage rankings require a baseline of at least 10 calls/day or 1,000 estimated tonnes/day. These are screening thresholds, not significance tests. Rankings follow the filters; they are not a global trade index. Colors use a ±5% neutral band, not a statistical-significance threshold. Reference rings are shown for passages and the selected port; marker sizes are bounded for legibility. The default globe shows up to 80 ranked matches plus the selected location. Enable Show all places for the full filtered map catalog. Measure, window and reference controls sit with the timeline.

Recent changes, historical context and disruption recovery answer different questions. Inspect the reference dates before interpreting a percentage. Historical ranges are descriptive, not statistical significance tests.
